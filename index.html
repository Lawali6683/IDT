About IDT

Who We Are

Intelligent Digital Technology is a Gusau-based tech company building real digital solutions for businesses, schools, and institutions across Nigeria.

Built in Gusau — Serving the World

IDT started with a simple mission: make professional digital technology accessible to Nigerian businesses. We design, build, and maintain websites, mobile apps, school portals, payroll systems, VTU platforms, AI tools, and security infrastructure.

Every project we deliver is crafted for performance, secured against threats, and scaled for growth. We use modern stacks like Supabase, MongoDB, Firebase, Cloudflare Workers, Flutter, React, and Python.
